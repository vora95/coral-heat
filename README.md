# Coral Heat — Make GTA 6

[![rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/vora95/coral-heat/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)
[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/vora95/coral-heat/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)

An open-world crime game in Coral Bay, a sun-soaked coastal city. Drive and steal cars, run 9 story missions, outrun the police at wanted levels 0–5, and build your empire with shops, safehouses, a garage, street races, taxi fares and deliveries — all rendered in a real 3D chase-cam.

Play it: open `game/index.html` in a browser, or run `node serve.ts` and visit http://localhost:8080.

Controls: WASD/arrows drive & walk, Shift nitro, Space handbrake, E interact, F fire, R radio, P pause, M mute.

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01
